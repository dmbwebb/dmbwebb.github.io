# Visitor notes for www.duncan-webb.com

Running notes on site traffic from GoatCounter. Add a dated section each time stats are pulled or a spike is explained. Data and chart: `visitors_daily.csv` and `visitors_over_time.png` (rebuild with `python3 pull_visitors.py && Rscript plot_visitors.R`).

## 2026-11-06

Tracking began on 31 July 2026. From then to 6 November there were 1,644 visitors and 558 tracked link clicks.

Monthly visitors:

- August: 270 (about 9 a day)
- September: 493 (about 16 a day)
- October: 763 (about 25 a day)
- 1 to 6 November: 116 (about 19 a day)

### Spike on 30 September 2026

30 September was the busiest day so far, with 140 visitors, and 1 October had 72. Across those two days (212 visits):

- About 84 visits came through Twitter/X short links, 46 of them from one tweet link (`t.co/9FCXvT9A4R`). About 95 came from Google, 4 from LinkedIn and 2 from Google Scholar.
- Almost all landed on the homepage. There were 43 CV downloads and 18 downloads of the Silence to Solidarity paper.
- 93 visitors were in the US, followed by France (26), Germany (17), Switzerland (12) and the UK (12).

JPE published Silence to Solidarity online on 22 September 2026, so the tweet was probably about the paper. Its author has not been identified, because the `t.co` link can't be opened without X access. That day's only site change was the homepage commit "Update homepage research focus to AI".
