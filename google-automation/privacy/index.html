Privacy policy

Duncan Webb operates this private, single-user automation for his own Google account. This policy describes the Google data it can access, why it uses that data, and how Duncan can revoke or delete access.

Google data the tool can access

Depending on the task Duncan authorizes, the tool can access:

  • Gmail messages, attachments, drafts, labels, and message metadata. Gmail modify access allows the tool to read messages and manage drafts or labels.
  • Google Calendar events and calendar metadata.
  • Google Contacts entries and related metadata.
  • Files and folders in Google Drive, including the contents of Google Docs and Google Sheets.
  • Google Cloud Identity and Access Management authorization used to obtain short-lived, read-only access for a Firestore export.

How Google data is used

The tool uses Google data to complete tasks that Duncan requests or has scheduled, such as finding correspondence, drafting replies, managing calendars, reading or updating documents and spreadsheets, organizing Drive files, and exporting research data for authorized local analysis. It does not sell Google data, use it for advertising, build advertising profiles, or use it for an unrelated purpose.

AI-assisted tasks

When Duncan deliberately runs an AI-assisted task, the minimum content needed to complete it may be processed by the AI provider he selected, currently Anthropic or OpenAI. That processing is limited to returning the requested result and is subject to the provider and account settings in use for that task. The tool does not intentionally use Google data to train or improve a general-purpose artificial intelligence or machine-learning model.

Storage and security

OAuth client information and refresh tokens are stored in the macOS login Keychain. Traffic to Google and other service providers uses HTTPS. A workflow may retain Google content or a derived output in a local project file or in private storage selected by Duncan when the task requires a saved result.

Retention and deletion

Credentials remain until Duncan revokes or deletes them. Saved files and derived outputs remain until Duncan deletes them under the retention rules of the relevant project or storage service. Duncan can revoke access from Google Account connections, delete the Keychain credentials and saved outputs, or contact dmbwebb@gmail.com for help.

Sharing

The tool shares Google data only with a service needed to complete a task Duncan requested, such as Google itself or the AI provider selected for an AI-assisted task. It does not disclose Google data to data brokers, advertisers, or the general public.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the tool's access or data practices change. Questions about this policy can be sent to Duncan Webb at dmbwebb@gmail.com.
